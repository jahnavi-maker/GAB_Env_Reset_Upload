Privacy Policy

GAB Environment Seeder · Last updated: 11 November 2026

The GAB Environment Seeder ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Deccan ("we", "us") to prepare and reset evaluation environments. The App uses Google APIs to populate and reset the contents of Google accounts that are enrolled in our evaluation program, with the authorization of the account holder. This policy explains what Google user data the App accesses and how we use it.

Information we access

With the account holder's explicit OAuth consent, the App accesses the following Google services for an enrolled account:

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/) — to add, modify, and remove email messages that make up the evaluation environment.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) — to add, modify, and remove files and folders that make up the evaluation environment.
  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar) — to add, modify, and remove calendar events that make up the evaluation environment.
  • Basic profile email (openid, userinfo.email) — to confirm which account was authorized.

How we use the information

We use this access solely to seed, reconcile, and reset the evaluation environment within the enrolled account — that is, to create the baseline content a task requires and to restore that baseline when the account is reset. We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as required to provide this functionality (the Google APIs themselves).

Data retention

The App stores an OAuth refresh token for each authorized account so it can perform resets on request, and a minimal operational record of each reset (account, timestamp, status). It does not retain copies of your email, files, or calendar contents beyond what is transiently processed to apply a reset. Authorization can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops further access.

Limited Use disclosure

GAB Environment Seeder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about data the App holds can be sent to divyasai@deccanexperts.us.
