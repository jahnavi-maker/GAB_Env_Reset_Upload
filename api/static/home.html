GAB Environment Seeder

Internal tooling for preparing and resetting evaluation environments.

GAB Environment Seeder is an internal application operated by Deccan. It prepares a consistent starting environment inside an enrolled Google account — seeding the Gmail, Drive, and Calendar content a given evaluation task requires — and restores that baseline when the account is reset between tasks.

What it does

  • Seeds a task's baseline email, files, and calendar events into an authorized account.
  • Lets the account holder reset their environment back to that baseline on demand.
  • Operates only on accounts whose holders have granted access through Google OAuth.
Access & consent. The App accesses Google data only after the account holder explicitly authorizes it, and only to perform the seeding/reset described above. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings.
